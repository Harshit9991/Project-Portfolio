# Project: Portfolio

An idea archive for a second, immersive personal website. Nothing here is built yet. This repo keeps the ideas, decisions and open questions so none get lost.

Last updated: 29 October 2026.

## The idea

A separate site for people who want to spend time getting to know who Harshit is, not just skim a CV.

- The existing professional portfolio (https://harshit-chebolu.vercel.app) stays exactly as it is, as the quick view.
- The new site links back to it with a prominent "Short portfolio" button.
- Primary audience: employers and other people who choose to explore.
- Inspiration: https://aarya.wtf - a coherent world with touchable objects. The goal is the same consistency, not a copy of its theme.

## Decisions so far

- It is a separate site from the professional portfolio. The current one is not replaced or changed.
- The experience should feel like one coherent world, in Harshit's own voice, narrative and interests.
- The art style is not chosen yet.
- Real objects from his life are a strong direction: digitise them and make them interactable.
- He may pay for a domain later. No domain has been chosen or bought.
- This repo is public. Ideas only: no site code, deployment or domain work has been approved.

## Working process

1. Discover the story and theme (current stage)
2. Choose content
3. Map the experience
4. Design desktop and mobile
5. Build a preview, after his go-ahead
6. QA and launch on its own domain, after approval

## Concept bank (ideas only, none approved)

Shared interaction idea: each element shows a small glimpse, then opens a readable story if the visitor wants more. They are modules to pick from, not a list to build.

1. **"It only has to work once."** His line next to a few crossed-out paths. Opening it reveals the Soulsborne reflection. Visitors never have to win a fight to read it.
2. **A curiosity trail.** F1 tyres, game theory, marginal costs and benefits as connected nodes. Each opens the question that pulled him deeper. Needs his actual notes or article.
3. **Small moments, collected.** An everyday memory paired with a short poem. Needs real poems and memories from him.
4. **A world he created.** A small original fantasy map opening into a fragment of a D&D world he wrote. Needs real writing, not invented scenery.
5. **A little optimisation puzzle.** An optional toy where improving one choice tightens another constraint, to show what he enjoys about min-maxing. Not a skill score or a test.

Strongest starting pair, in Instinct's view: concepts 1 and 2.

## Digitising real objects (new direction)

Harshit wants to take real products and objects around him into the digital world and make them interactable.

- These become digital assets. How each one behaves is a separate decision.
- Not everything needs to be 3D. Options per object: a cut-out photo that opens, tilts or reveals a story; a few photographed views; a 3D model if rotating and inspecting really matters.
- No 3D capture is promised.
- Prefer his actual objects over decorative props.
- First object and photo: not chosen yet.

Assets live in [`assets/`](assets/README.md).

## Guardrails

- No fake skill stats, completion gates or forced gameplay.
- No autoplay audio.
- Normal navigation, readable text on mobile, reduced-motion support and a clear text fallback for every interactive piece.
- The "Short portfolio" button is always easy to find.
- Do not invent stories, attempt counts or claims his record does not support.
